# Pricing and monetization

## Now: free during validation

- Verdarium is free with no limits while we validate the archive-first positioning.
- An optional **Buy me a coffee** link lets appreciative collectors support the project (`specs/002-support-link.md`). Clicks are a weak but useful willingness-to-pay signal.
- Stripe is already the platform for provisioning (Stripe Projects). If and when paid plans arrive, Stripe Billing is the natural choice.

## Later: premium for larger collections (hypothesis)

A reasonably priced premium tier aimed at serious collectors and professionals. Candidate levers:

- Specimen count above a generous free threshold (the threshold is to be decided and should comfortably fit a casual collection)
- Advanced archive features: provenance and lineage, care history insights, printable specimen sheets, additional illustration plates
- Shared or household editing

**Open questions:** the free threshold, whether billing is monthly or annual, the price point, and whether conservatories and professionals need their own plan. Decide these only after validation signals from `ROADMAP.md` come in.

## Principles

- Never paywall a collector's existing records. Export always stays free.
- Premium should feel like expanding the archive, not unlocking care basics.
