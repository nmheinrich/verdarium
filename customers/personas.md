# Personas

These are working hypotheses. Update them as interviews come in.

## 1. The serious collector (primary)

- 40–200+ plants, often a specialty such as aroids, hoyas, orchids, or succulents
- Tracks them today in a spreadsheet, a notes app, nursery tags, and a camera roll
- Cares about provenance: where the plant came from, when, which cultivar, and what it cost
- Proud of the collection and shows it off
- **Wins with Verdarium:** structured records, a whole-collection view, and a beautiful archive to share
- **Risk:** needs bulk entry and import. Adding 100 specimens by hand is a barrier.

## 2. The design-conscious plant owner

- 15–40 plants, and a strong interior and aesthetic sensibility
- Put off by utilitarian or gamified plant apps
- **Wins:** the visual design, the themes, and gentle care that doesn't nag
- **Risk:** may not need deep metadata, so the first-run experience must feel light

## 3. The shared household

- Two or more people caring for one collection, and they forget who watered what
- **Wins:** care recorded on the tile with history, and a shared view
- **Risk:** today's sharing is read-only, so shared editing is on the "Later" list

## 4. The specialist or professional

- Small conservatories, specialty growers, interior plant professionals
- Manages many specimens across locations and needs records they can rely on
- **Wins:** structured catalog, locations, care history
- **Risk:** likely needs multi-location support and team features, which makes them the best candidates for a future premium tier
