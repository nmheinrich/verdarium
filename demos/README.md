# Demos

Marketing material: flow clips to post online, screenshots, and shot lists.

## Structure

| Folder | Contents |
|---|---|
| `screenshots/` | Final, posted-quality stills (PNG or WebP, under 1 MB each) |
| `clips/` | Final short flow clips (MP4 or GIF, under 10 MB each; keep it short) |
| `raw/` | Raw screen recordings and outtakes. **Gitignored** |

Naming: `YYYY-MM-DD-<flow>-<theme>-<device>.<ext>`, e.g. `2026-10-01-record-care-archive-mobile.mp4`.

## Rules

- Use the **demo collection** (a curated sample archive), never a real customer's data. The repo is public.
- Sign in with a dedicated demo account, not a personal one.
- Hide the browser chrome, notifications, and personal bookmarks.
- Capture each hero shot in Archive (the default) and at least one alternate theme.
- Clips are 5–15 seconds, show one idea each, and have no voiceover.
